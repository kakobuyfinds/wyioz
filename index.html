Fit / Article 01

SuperBuy sizing guide: start with something that fits

Lay a comfortable garment flat and record chest width, shoulder width, body length and sleeve length. Compare these with the specific listing's size chart.

Check whether dimensions describe the body or the garment, and whether width is measured flat or around. For shoes, use the seller's stated foot-length guidance. Avoid assuming that every brand or seller uses the same sizing.

  • For a flat garment measurement method, smooth the fabric without stretching it.
  • Record centimeters and inches carefully; 1 inch equals 2.54 centimeters.
  • For a relaxed fit clothing comparison, use a reference piece with the silhouette you want.

Useful record: your measurements, the listing chart and the seller's stated tolerance.

Inspection / Article 02

SuperBuy QC photos: ask a question before requesting an angle

Identify the detail you need to confirm before requesting a photograph. Ask for the size label and selected color, close views of stitching or closures, and a tape measurement when fit is uncertain.

Photos can reveal visible condition but do not prove authenticity, long-term durability or exact material composition.

  • A QC photo comparison guide should separate a wrong variant from a lighting difference.
  • A clothing seam inspection needs a close, focused photograph of the area in question.
  • For a size label verification, compare the label with your order record and measurements.

Useful request: “Please show the chest width laid flat, with both tape endpoints visible.”

Discovery / Article 03

SuperBuy finds: keep the listing and the variant together

Save the source listing, selected variant, size chart and a dated note together. Reopen the listing before payment and compare the title, photos, seller and available options.

Do not substitute a different product solely because its thumbnail looks similar. A product link availability check only confirms that a page opens; it does not establish stock or fulfillment.

  • Use a seller return policy checklist before ordering unfamiliar sizing.
  • For a fashion accessories shortlist, record dimensions and closure type.
  • Distinguish seller photographs from photos of the exact item being shipped.

Useful record: one listing, one selected option and one clear reason for choosing it.

Preparation / Article 04

SuperBuy shipping checklist: plan the package before the parcel

Record item weight, packaging needs and destination restrictions before choosing a delivery service. Compare current route terms and checkout estimates.

Consider protection for structured accessories, moisture protection for clothing and the space occupied by shoe boxes. Confirm the total cost for your destination before paying.

  • A parcel packaging comparison should balance protection with unnecessary volume.
  • A shipping weight estimate depends on the route's current billing method.
  • A total shopping cost checklist includes applicable fees, delivery, taxes and duties.

Useful question: what changes if you keep the original packaging?

When browsing brand-led styles: Nike footwear, Louis Vuitton accessories, Gucci clothing, Hermès scarves and Dior headwear each require item-specific research. A familiar name, monogram or logo is not evidence of authenticity. Check provenance and use an authorized retailer when authenticity is essential.

Short answers to the questions that matter when moving from a discovery page to an actual listing.

What is a SuperBuy spreadsheet?

A SuperBuy spreadsheet is a product-discovery list that organizes shopping references by category, often with listing links, sizing notes and QC references. An independent list does not guarantee stock, seller quality or authenticity.

Where do the links on this page go?

The ten category links open their corresponding CAFANS categories. Every other navigation link opens https://www.cafans.com. This page does not provide a SuperBuy checkout or link converter.

Should I choose my usual clothing size?

Use the individual seller's measurement chart and compare it with a garment that already fits. Letter sizes and regional conversions alone are not enough to predict fit.

Can QC photos prove that an item is authentic?

No. QC photos can help assess visible condition, selected options and measurements. They do not independently establish authenticity, material composition or long-term performance.

How should I estimate the total shopping cost?

Record the item cost and any applicable domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping, taxes and duties. Use current destination-specific checkout information rather than a third-party example.

Does this page represent SuperBuy or the pictured brands?

No. This is an independent editorial guide. Brand names and illustrative photographs do not imply affiliation, endorsement, authenticated inventory or product availability.
